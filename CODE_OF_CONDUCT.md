# Code of Conduct

RailKeeper welcomes respectful, technically constructive participation.

Participants must communicate professionally, discuss ideas rather than personal attributes, and
respect different experience levels and perspectives. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

Report serious conduct concerns confidentially to the maintainer `@ichwars` through RailKeeper's
[private GitHub reporting form](https://github.com/ichwars/RailKeeper/security/advisories/new).
State clearly that the report concerns community conduct rather than a software vulnerability.
Do not publish sensitive details in an issue or discussion.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or exclude a
participant when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior, its impact, and any
previous incidents. Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
