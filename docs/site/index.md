## See how RailKeeper works

Start with one locomotive or import an existing list.
You do not need a command station to manage your collection.
The pictures show synthetic sample data in a separate demo installation.

### 1. Search your collection

Find a model and open its record. Vehicles from a shared box can be grouped as a set.

<GuideFigure name="vehicles" alt="Vehicle inventory with a demo locomotive, coaches and an expandable set." caption="Stock, filters and vehicle sets at a glance. Click for the full picture." />

### 2. Add pictures and documents

Keep photos and documents with the vehicle so that its manual is easy to find later.

<GuideFigure name="media" alt="Vehicle editor with picture and attachment sections and upload buttons." caption="Manage pictures and attachments directly on the vehicle." />

### 3. Plan the next service

Record completed work and what still needs doing.

<GuideFigure name="maintenance" alt="Vehicle maintenance section showing a planned cleaning entry." caption="Plan maintenance and look up its history on the vehicle." />

## Your first try

The [tester quick start](try) takes you from startup through importing two sample vehicles
to adding a third vehicle with a picture. The downloads and a short checklist are on the same page.

Windows runs from a ZIP. AMD64 and ARM64 Docker images are available for your home server.
Use the [handbook](guide/) for the next tasks and the
[installation guide](administration/deployment-configuration) for ongoing operation.
The pictures show **v0.2.0** with synthetic demo data. See the
[release notes](https://github.com/ichwars/RailKeeper/releases/tag/v0.2.0) and
[changelog](https://github.com/ichwars/RailKeeper/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#020---2026-11-09) for this version's scope.
